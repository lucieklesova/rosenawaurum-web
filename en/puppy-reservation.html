Puppy reservation

Are you interested in a puppy from us? I would be happy to get to know you and discuss whether a Golden Retriever from Rosenaw Aurum could be the right companion for your family.

The first step is to complete our non-binding contact form. Afterwards, we can speak by phone, meet in person or arrange a video call. We can also communicate in English.

How does the process work?

  1. Complete the contact formTell me about yourself, your family, your experience with dogs and your expectations.
  2. We get to know each otherWe speak by phone or meet in person or online and discuss your plans and questions.
  3. Non-binding registrationBefore the puppies are born, this is not a binding reservation of a particular dog. After the litter is born, I will contact interested families by phone.
  4. Visit and choose a puppyIf possible, we will meet in person and choose one or two puppies according to their character, potential and your family’s lifestyle.
Important: Being added to the waiting list does not reserve a particular puppy. My aim is to find the right family for every puppy.

Interested?

Please write to me through the contact form. I look forward to meeting you!
